#Privacy Policy

The Harvest Finance website does not request, collect or store any personal user information. When a user connects their wallet to the Harvest Finance website interface, public data on the Ethereum blockchain is analyzed by the website to generate various metrics for the benefit of the user, but this publicly available information is not stored or retained by Harvest Finance.


The user should be mindful that usage of 3rd party wallet applications such as MetaMask or WalletConnect may independently collect user data for its own purposes, but these 3rd party applications are not controlled by the Harvest Finance website and therefore are outside the scope of this privacy policy. Please refer to those 3rd party applications Privacy Policies for further information.
